Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your personal data. You send the request with one click - from your own mailbox, so we know it comes from the owner of the address.

What we delete

Contact and profile data, yours and your child's - names, addresses, phone numbers, notes - will disappear from our database or be irreversibly anonymised.

What the law does not let us delete

  • accounting documents (invoices, payment confirmations) - the rules require us to keep them for 5 years,
  • records of consents and of safety from the period when a child was in our care.

These documents do lose their link to your identity wherever that is possible.

How long it takes

We will confirm we received the request by email and carry it out without undue delay, within 30 days at the latest.
